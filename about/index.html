Our nursery

A place where little minds can flourish.

Welcome to Little Explorers Nursery Telford. We believe that the early years of childhood are a special time filled with discovery, imagination and exciting opportunities.

Our aim is to provide a welcoming and supportive environment where children can feel comfortable being themselves, develop their confidence and enjoy discovering new things.

We recognise that every child is different. Their individual interests, personalities and experiences all play an important part in their journey of learning and development.

Our approach

Helping every child take their next step.

We believe that children should be encouraged to explore, express themselves and develop at their own pace. Our approach is built around creating positive experiences and opportunities for every little explorer.

🌈

Every Child Is Unique

Every child brings something special. We value individual personalities, interests and abilities, recognising that everyone develops in their own way.

🧩

Learning Through Play

Play can open the door to discovery. We value opportunities for children to experiment, imagine, investigate and develop new skills through experiences.

💚

Feeling Supported

Feeling safe, welcome and understood matters. We believe positive relationships help children build confidence and enjoy their nursery experience.

What we believe

Little steps can lead to wonderful things.

A child's early experiences can help shape the way they understand themselves and the world around them. We believe those experiences should be filled with encouragement, curiosity and opportunities to discover.

Whether it is exploring something for the first time, developing a new interest or building a friendship, every little milestone deserves to be celebrated.

We want Little Explorers to be a place where children are encouraged to be curious, develop their confidence and enjoy being themselves.
